Guitar 001

Fully hand made, custom designed, semi-hollow elextric guitar. Flame maple top. Bubinga body. Sapele Neck. Chechen fretboard and headstock veneer. Compound radius fretboard. Schaller bridge and locking tuners. Hand cut bone nut. Schaller knobs. Gator Case hard case included in purchase price.
